## Main learning points

- Clarify what the patient means by “dizzy”: **vertigo** vs **presyncope** vs **imbalance**.
- Identify **red flags** (stroke/TIA features, new focal neuro deficit, severe headache, neck pain after trauma, acute hearing loss, syncope, exertional symptoms).
- Use targeted bedside tests when vertigo is likely (e.g. **Dix–Hallpike**; consider HINTS only if trained and in the right setting).
- Always safety-net.

## Management summary (one page)

1) Categorise symptom pattern (vertigo / presyncope / disequilibrium)
2) Screen red flags → same-day ED/acute assessment
3) If BPPV likely: positional testing → Epley manoeuvre + advice
4) If vestibular neuritis likely: symptomatic treatment + follow-up
